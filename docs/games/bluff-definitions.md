# Bluff Definitions

Here's a real word nobody knows. Make up what it means, and spot the real meaning. 3–12 players.

## The short version
- Each round shows a real, uncommon English word. Not its meaning.
- You write a meaning that could pass for the real one. It goes on the board with the real meaning, shuffled.
- Everyone votes for the one they think is real, but not their own.
- Find the real meaning: 2 points. Fool someone with your fake: 1 point per person fooled.
- Most points after the last round wins.

## How it plays
1. **Write (60 seconds).** You see "An uncommon English word" and the word. Type a made-up meaning (15 to 180 characters) and tap **Send**. A counter shows how long yours is. The round moves on early once everyone who's here has sent one.
2. **Vote (30 seconds).** The real meaning and all the fakes appear in random order with no names. Your own is tagged "yours" and can't be picked. Tap another to vote. You can change your vote until everyone has voted or the clock ends.
3. **The reveal (12 seconds).** The real meaning is marked "Real", each fake shows "Fake by <name>", and each option lists who it fooled or who found it. Points land, with a short note on where the word comes from.
4. The next round starts by itself. The host can tap **Next round** to skip the wait (**Finish** on the last round).

## Winning and scoring
- Voting for the real meaning: 2 points.
- Each player who votes for your fake: 1 point to you.
- After the last round, the highest score wins. Ties share the win.
- If too few players are left, the game ends with no winner.

## Settings
Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rounds | 3, **5** | How many words you play. The lobby shows a rough maximum time. |

## Making everything look alike
Every option on the board is shown the same way, the real one too: first letter lowercased, trailing full stops, "!" and "?" removed, curly quotes straightened. So there's no point agonising over punctuation or capitals. They won't give you away.

## What your fake can't be
Fakes are turned away if they:
- are under 15 characters;
- are nearly the real meaning ("That's the real meaning. Bluff."): same text, a few letters apart, one inside the other, or about 70% of the same words;
- are nearly the same as another player's ("Someone wrote nearly the same. Try another.").

You get the reason for the first 3 refusals each round. After that, the only reply is "Try something else." That's deliberate, so refusals can't be used to fish for the answer.

## On your screen
- **The card:** the word during writing and voting. The real meaning appears at the reveal, plus a short note on the word.
- **Timer:** seconds left and a bar.
- **Input and Send:** during writing. Enter sends. After sending, you see "Yours: <text>" and who we're waiting for.
- **Options:** the board during voting. "Voted. You can change it." shows once you've picked.
- **Players:** scores, plus who has finished the current step.
- **Sounds:** a tick near the end of the clock, a shuffle when a round starts, a chime and buzz when voting opens, a ding or a thud at the reveal depending on whether you found the real one. Tapping a vote buzzes briefly.

## When things go sideways
- **Time runs out while writing:** anyone who hasn't sent sits the round out ("Sat this one out"). They can still vote.
- **Nobody writes anything:** "No fakes this round." No vote, straight to the reveal.
- **Time runs out while voting:** anyone who didn't vote scores nothing from voting. Fakes can still earn votes.
- **Someone disconnects:** they don't hold up a phase. The round moves on once everyone who's connected has finished. The host can skip someone who's holding things up.
- **Someone is removed by the host:** what they wrote stays on the board, but their vote is dropped. If fewer than 3 people are left, the game ends.
- **Play again:** starts a new game with the same settings. Words from the last game aren't picked again straight away.

## Tips
- The real definition is usually plain and a bit dry. A fake that sounds like a dictionary entry will pull votes.
- Mimic the way the real ones are written: short, one clause, no jokes.
