# Fake Word

Here's what a real word means. Make up a word that could mean it, and spot the real one. 3–12 players.

## The short version
- Each round shows the meaning of a real, uncommon English word. Not the word.
- You invent a word that could plausibly mean it. Your fake goes on the board alongside the real one, shuffled.
- Everyone votes for the one they think is real, but not their own.
- Find the real word: 2 points. Fool someone with your fake: 1 point per person fooled.
- Most points after the last round wins.

## How it plays
1. **Write (45 seconds).** You see "An uncommon English word. Its meaning:" and the meaning. Type a made-up word (3 to 18 letters, a to z only) and tap **Send**. The round moves on early once everyone who's here has sent one.
2. **Vote (30 seconds).** The real word and all the fakes appear in random order with no names. Your own fake is tagged "yours" and can't be picked. Tap another to vote. You can change your vote until everyone has voted or the clock ends.
3. **The reveal (12 seconds).** The real word is marked "Real", each fake shows "Fake by <name>", and each option lists who it fooled or who found it. Points land, and there's a short note on where the word comes from.
4. The next round starts by itself. The host can tap **Next round** to skip the wait (**Finish** on the last round).

## Winning and scoring
- Voting for the real word: 2 points.
- Each player who votes for your fake: 1 point to you.
- After the last round, the highest score wins. Ties share the win.
- If too few players are left, the game ends with no winner.

## Settings
Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rounds | 3, **5** | How many words you play. The lobby shows a rough maximum time. |

## What your fake can't be
Fakes are turned away if they:
- are not 3–18 letters, a to z only;
- are a real dictionary word, or within two letter edits of the real one;
- match a fake someone else already sent ("Someone's already made that one up").

You get the reason for the first 3 refusals each round. After that, the only reply is "Try something else." That's deliberate, so nobody can use refusals to fish for the answer. The wording for a real or near-real word doesn't say which of the two it was.

## On your screen
- **The card:** the meaning during writing and voting. The word itself appears at the reveal, plus a short note on its origin.
- **Timer:** seconds left and a bar.
- **Input and Send:** during writing. After sending, you see "Yours: <word>" and who we're waiting for.
- **Options:** the board during voting. "Voted. You can change it." shows once you've picked.
- **Players:** scores, plus who has finished the current step.
- **Sounds:** a tick near the end of the clock, a shuffle when a round starts, a chime and buzz when voting opens, a ding or a thud at the reveal depending on whether you found the real one. Tapping a vote buzzes briefly.

## When things go sideways
- **Time runs out while writing:** anyone who hasn't sent sits the round out ("Sat this one out"). They can still vote.
- **Nobody writes anything:** "No fakes this round." No vote, straight to the reveal.
- **Time runs out while voting:** anyone who didn't vote scores nothing from voting. Fakes can still earn votes.
- **Someone disconnects:** they don't hold up a phase. The round moves on once everyone who's connected has finished. The host can skip someone who's holding things up.
- **Someone is removed by the host:** what they wrote stays on the board, but their vote is dropped. If fewer than 3 people are left, the game ends.
- **Play again:** starts a new game with the same settings. Words from the last game aren't picked again straight away.

## Tips
- Real words tend to follow word-building patterns. A fake that looks like one will pull votes.
- Don't stick yours out by being too silly or too perfect.
