# Oh Hell

Hand sizes change every round. Bid exactly how many tricks you'll take: one more or one fewer scores nothing. 3–7 players.

## The short version

- Everyone gets the same number of cards. That number climbs and falls over the game: 1, 2, 3 … 7 … 3, 2, 1 by default.
- After the deal, the next card is turned up. Its suit is trump.
- Everyone bids how many tricks they'll take. The dealer goes last and can't pick the number that makes the bids add up, so someone has to miss.
- Take exactly your bid: 10 points plus the bid. Miss by one either way: nothing.

## How it plays

1. The dealer deals one card at a time from their left, then turns up the next card. Its suit is trump for the round. The dealer moves one seat left each round.
2. **Bidding.** Starting left of the dealer, each player bids 0 up to the hand size. Bids are public as they're made, and the table shows "Bids so far: 4 of 6".
3. The dealer bids last and can't bid the number that would make the total equal the hand size. Try it and you're told why: "Can't bid 2: the bids would add up to 3 tricks".
4. **Play.** The player left of the dealer leads the first trick. Everyone plays one card, going left. You must follow the suit led if you can. If you can't, play anything, trump included.
5. The highest trump takes the trick. If no trump was played, the highest card of the suit led does. Aces are high. Off-suit cards never win, however high.
6. The finished trick stays up for a moment so everyone sees who took it, then sweeps to the winner, who leads the next one.
7. When the hands are empty, the round's scored and a summary shows bids against tricks. It moves on after five seconds, or as soon as everyone taps **Ready**.

## Winning and scoring

- Take exactly what you bid: **10 + your bid**. Bidding 0 and taking none is worth 10.
- Miss and score **0**, or lose a point per trick over or under if the host picked "Costs the difference".
- After the last round, the highest total wins. Ties share it. The results list everyone by total.

## Settings

Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Schedule | **Up and down**, Down only, Up only | Hand sizes 1 → biggest → 1, biggest → 1, or 1 → biggest. |
| Biggest hand | **7**, 10, Most the deck allows | Capped so there's always a card left to turn up: 17 with 3 players, 7 with 7. The hint shows how many rounds that makes, e.g. "13 rounds". |
| Dealer's restriction | **On**, Off | On: the bids can't add up to the cards in hand, so someone misses. Off: they can. |
| Missing a bid | **Scores 0**, Costs the difference | Costs the difference: bid 2, take 4, lose 2 points. |

## On your screen

- **Trump and the round.** The turned-up card, "Round 4 of 13 · 4 cards", and a strip of bars, one per round, as tall as its hand. Played rounds are faded, the current one's lit.
- **Other players.** A chip each: name, a **D** on the dealer, score, cards left, and "bid 2 · won 1". It goes lime when they've hit their bid exactly and red once they're over.
- **Bidding.** The table shows "Bids so far" against the hand size. On your bid, a row of numbers sits above your hand; the dealer's forbidden number is struck out and can't be pressed. Tap a number, then tap it again (or press **Bid N**) to send it.
- **The trick.** One slot per player in play order, with names under the cards. The one that takes it lifts and the rest dim. **Last trick** opens the previous trick.
- **Your hand.** Sorted by suit, trump first, low to high. Trump cards have a lime stripe. On your turn, cards you can't play are dimmed (the server says which). Tap a card to lift it, then tap it again or press **Play** to put it down. Two taps on purpose: there's no taking a card back.
- **Round summary.** Bid, won, points and total for everyone, a tick by whoever's ready, and the countdown.
- Sounds and vibration cue the deal, plays, tricks you take, your turn, and whether you made your bid.

## When things go sideways

- **Playing the wrong suit.** Dimmed cards can't be played. Tapping one tells you which suit to follow.
- **Disconnected player.** The game waits for them; their chip says "reconnecting…". The host can skip or remove them.
- **Idle player.** The host gets a "Skip" button after a while. A skip bids 0 for them (1 if 0 is the dealer's forbidden bid), or plays their lowest legal card, keeping trump back if it can.
- **The host removes a player.** Scores stay. The round in progress is redealt at the same hand size, bids and all. Later rounds follow what the new player count allows, carrying on in the same direction. If fewer than 3 are left, the game ends and ranks by score.
- **Game over.** The host sees Rematch and Back to lobby; everyone else sees Play again.

## Tips

- Bidding 0 in a one-card round is the classic trap: it's 10 points if your card is low, and the dealer is the one stuck guessing.
- A high card in a short suit is a liability when you've bid low. Get rid of it the first time you can't follow.
- Watch "Bids so far". If it's under the hand size, someone's going to take tricks they didn't want.
