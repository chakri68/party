# Pass the Bomb

Type a word with the letters on the bomb to pass it on. Nobody knows how long the fuse is. 2–12 players.

## The short version
- One player holds a lit bomb showing a few letters, like ING or TR.
- On your go, type a real word containing those letters, in that order. A good word sends the bomb to the next player with new letters.
- The fuse is random and secret, and it keeps burning while the bomb moves. Whoever holds it when it goes off loses a life.
- Last player standing wins.

## How it plays
1. A round starts with a fresh fuse and the bomb in someone's hands. Round 1 goes to the dealer's left. After a bang, the player who just blew up starts the next round (or the next living seat along, if they're out).
2. The holder sees "Your go" and what's needed, for example "a word with ING". Everyone else sees who has it.
3. Type a word and tap **Pass**. If it fits, it goes on the played list and the bomb moves to the next living player, who gets new letters. The fuse doesn't reset.
4. A word that doesn't fit shakes the input and says why. Nothing is lost: keep trying until the fuse goes.
5. When the fuse runs out, the holder loses a life. The bang takes 4 seconds to clear, then the next round starts.
6. Players with no lives left are out. The game ends when one player is left.

A word counts if it:
- is letters only, 3 letters at least (30 at most);
- contains the bomb's letters together and in order (SINGER and RUNNING both have ING);
- hasn't been played yet this game, by anyone;
- is in the dictionary (or gets through a room vote, see below).

Case doesn't matter. Names aren't words.

## Winning and scoring
There are no points. You win by being the last player with a life. The results list shows the winner first, then everyone else by how long they lasted ("first out", "out 2nd" and so on), with anyone who left the room last.

If you're typing when the fuse goes, you're too late. A word sent after the fuse ends is rejected with "Too late."

## Settings
Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lives | 1, **2**, 3 | How many bangs you can take. |
| Fuse | Short, **Normal**, Long | Each round's fuse is random within a range: Short 6–18 s, Normal 10–30 s, Long 18–45 s. |
| Letters go | **Anywhere**, At the end | At the end means the word has to end with the letters (RUNNING, not SINGER). |
| Bomb shows | **Letters as they are**, Letters backwards | Backwards shows GNI on the bomb; you still type a word with ING. The prompt always names the letters to type. |
| Word length | **3 or more**, 5 to 10 | Limits words to 5–10 letters. |
| Words from | **Anywhere**, A category | Words must come from the bomb's category: Animals, Food, Body, Home, Nature or Jobs, one at a time. Letters are then taken from that category's own words. |
| Unknown words | **Room votes**, Rejected, Allowed | What happens to a word that fits but isn't known (see below). |

Any mode that would leave too few words to play is marked "(too few words)" in the lobby. Some combinations are thin; the lobby warns when fewer than 150 words fit.

## Unknown words
This covers a word that fits the letters but isn't in the dictionary (or isn't on the category list, in category mode).

- **Room votes:** the fuse freezes and everyone else who's alive and connected gets 4 seconds to tap **Allow** or **Reject**. It's thrown out only if more than half of them say Reject; a tie lets it through. Voting ends early once the result can't change. You see who voted, but not how, until it closes. Then everyone sees the tally. The fuse picks up where it stopped. If it's rejected, the holder keeps the bomb and can try another word. A word the room has rejected can't be tried again. A holder gets 2 votes per hold, after which unknown words just bounce.
- **Rejected:** unknown words bounce straight away.
- **Allowed:** anything that fits goes.

If there's nobody else to ask (everyone else is out or disconnected), the word goes through.

## On your screen
- **The bomb:** the letters in the middle, in capitals. In category mode the category name sits beneath it.
- **Mode chips:** at the top, End, Backwards, 5–10 letters and Category show whichever are on.
- **Top bar:** the round number and how many words have been played.
- **Status line:** "Your go: ..." if you hold it, otherwise "<name> has it".
- **Typing line:** what the holder is typing shows live to everyone, with the bomb's letters highlighted. A bounced word shakes there too.
- **Input and Pass button:** only live for the holder. Out, or watching, and the box is hidden.
- **Last word:** "<name> played WORD".
- **Players:** lives as pips, a highlight on whoever holds the bomb, and "out" or "left" tags.
- **Sounds:** a tick every second while the fuse burns (the same tick whether the fuse is long or short), a chime when the bomb comes to you, a bang on the boom. Your phone buzzes on your go and on a bang.

## When things go sideways
- **Nobody can solve it:** the fuse still runs out. That's the game.
- **Someone disconnects:** they're passed over when the bomb moves, as long as someone else is here. If they're holding it, the fuse keeps burning on them. The host can skip a dropped holder.
- **Someone is removed by the host:** they're out without losing to a bang. If they held the bomb, it passes on with the fuse still lit. If they had a vote open, it's called off.
- **A voter drops mid-vote:** they stop counting, which can settle the vote.
- **Down to one player:** the game ends at once.
- **Play again:** starts a new game with the same settings. Lives, used words and rejected words all reset.

## Tips
- Easy words burn the clock too, so a short word you're sure of beats a long one you're not.
- Played words are gone for everyone. Plurals and endings of a played word are different words.
