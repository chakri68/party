# Crazy Eights

Match the suit or the rank. Eights are wild. First to empty their hand wins. 2–10 players.

## The short version

- Everyone gets 5 cards. The rest is the stock, and its top card is turned up to start the pile.
- On your turn, play a card that matches the top card's suit or rank, or an eight.
- Can't play? Draw until you can. You may draw even when you could play.
- First to empty their hand wins, and collects points for what's left in everyone else's.

## How it plays

1. Cards are dealt one at a time, clockwise from the dealer's left. One card from the stock is turned face up as the starter. If that's an eight, it gets buried in the stock and the next card is turned.
2. The player left of the dealer goes first.
3. Play a card of the same suit or the same rank as the top card.
4. An eight goes on anything. When you play one, you call a suit from a picker. The next player has to follow that suit, or play an eight of their own.
5. If you want to draw, press **Draw**. You take one card from the stock and it's still your turn: play something, or draw again.
6. If the stock is empty and you can't play, you pass. Nobody asks you to.
7. When someone plays their last card, the game ends.

## Winning and scoring

The player who empties their hand wins. They score the cards left in everyone else's hands:

| Card | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Eight | 50 |
| Ten, Jack, Queen, King | 10 |
| Ace | 1 |
| 2 to 9 (except eight) | face value |

If the stock is gone and nobody can play, the hand is dead: the lowest total in hand wins (ties share it). The results screen shows each player's points in hand, or the winner's total.

## Settings

Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decks | **Auto**, 1 deck, 2 decks | Auto uses 1 deck up to 5 players, 2 beyond. The lobby shows how many cards end up in the stock. If you pick 1 deck and it's too few for the table, the game uses 2. |
| Empty stock | **Pass**, Reshuffle the pile | Pass: once the stock is gone, nobody can draw. Reshuffle the pile: the pile, minus its top card, becomes the new stock. |

## On your screen

- **The table.** The stock on one side (with its count), the pile with its top card on the other. After an eight, a badge shows the called suit.
- **Other players.** Each shows a card count. A "D" marks the dealer.
- **Your hand.** Playable cards are highlighted. Eights sit at the end of your hand.
  - On a mouse, click a card to play it.
  - On touch, tap to select, then tap again or press **Play**.
- **Call a suit.** Playing an eight opens the picker. **Cancel** takes the eight back.
- **Draw.** Always available on your turn while there's something to draw. It's the main button when you have nothing to play.
- You'll see "You drew ..." for cards you pick up. Other players only see that you drew.
- Sounds and vibration cue the deal, plays, draws, shuffles, your turn and the ending.

## When things go sideways

- **You can't move.** Nothing to play and nothing to draw means you pass automatically, with a "Nothing to play" bubble.
- **Idle or disconnected player.** The host gets a "Skip" button after a while. A skip passes that player's turn.
- **The host removes a player.** Their cards go under the stock, so they stay in the game. If only one player is left, that player wins.
- **Game over.** The host sees Rematch and Back to lobby; everyone else sees Play again.

## Tips

- An eight still in your hand is 50 points to whoever goes out.
- Drawing is optional even when you can play, but it only adds to your hand.
