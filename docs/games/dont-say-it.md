# Don't Say It

Two teams. Describe the word without the five words that would make it easy. 4–12 players.

## The short version
- Two teams, Amber and Teal, take turns. On your team's turn one player describes a word out loud and the rest of the team guesses.
- Every card has the word and five more you can't say. The other team sees the card too, and buzzes if they hear a slip.
- Got it: +1. Skip: 0. Oops: -1. A team's score never drops below 0.
- Most points after the last turn wins. A tie gets one more turn each.

## How it plays
1. **Pick sides.** The lobby deals you into teams. Anyone can tap **Switch to <team>** as long as it keeps the teams within one player of each other. The host can **Shuffle** and taps **Start** when it's right. Each team needs at least 2 players.
2. **Ready.** One of the teams goes first (picked at random, then they alternate). The describer is whoever on that team has described the fewest turns so far (ties by seat order, connected players first). They tap **Start** when the team's listening.
3. **The turn.** The clock starts and the describer sees the card: the word, and under "Don't say", the five forbidden words. The describer talks, out loud, and their teammates shout guesses. No rhymes, no spelling, no pointing at things.
4. **Marking cards.** The describer taps:
   - **Got it** when the team gets it (+1).
   - **Skip** to pass on the card (0).
   - **Oops** if they said a forbidden word themselves (-1).
   Each tap deals the next card.
5. **Buzz.** The other team sees the card too. If they hear a slip, anyone on that team taps **Buzz**. The turn clock freezes (see below).
6. **Turn over.** When the clock ends, a summary shows every card played and the points the turn moved the score by. It moves on after 8 seconds, or the host can tap **Next turn**.

## Buzzes
When someone on the other team taps **Buzz**, the turn clock freezes and the describer gets 20 seconds to answer:
- **Fair (-1):** "yes, I slipped". The card scores -1 and the next one is dealt.
- **Nope:** they dispute it. The buzzing team's captain gets 20 seconds to decide.
  - **Uphold (-1):** the buzz stands. The card scores -1.
  - **Drop:** the buzz is dropped and the card carries on.

If the describer says nothing for 20 seconds, it goes to the captain anyway. If the captain says nothing for 20 seconds, the buzz is dropped. A buzz needs a yes. The captain is the first player in seat order on the buzzing team who is connected.

## Winning and scoring
- A team's score is the sum of its cards, floored at 0 after every change.
- After the last turn, the higher score wins. The result says "Amber win, 7 to 4" or similar.
- A tie plays one extra turn each (sudden death). If it's still tied after that, it's a draw and both teams win.

## Settings
Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Turn length | 45 s, **60 s**, 90 s | How long each turn's clock runs. |
| Turns per team | 2, **3**, 5 | Turns each team gets. Total turns is double this. The lobby shows a rough time estimate. |

## On your screen
- **Your role** changes each turn:
  - *Describer:* you see the card and the **Got it**, **Skip** and **Oops** buttons.
  - *Guesser* (on the describing team): you see "You're guessing" and "Shout it out", and the last card's result. The card stays hidden.
  - *Watcher* (on the other team): you see the card, and a **Buzz** button.
- **Header:** "Turn 3 of 6", or "Sudden death".
- **Teams:** both scores, members, and a highlight on whose turn it is.
- **This turn's cards:** a running list of results (Got it, Skipped, Oops, Buzzed, Buzz upheld, Time, Turn ended).
- **Timer:** the turn clock with a bar. It freezes during a dispute and shows the time left on the 20-second dispute clock.
- **Sounds:** a tick near the end of the clock, a chime when it's your turn to describe, a ding for Got it, a thud for Oops and buzzes. Your phone buzzes on your turn and on a buzz.

## When things go sideways
- **Clock runs out with a card in hand:** that card scores 0 (shown as "Time").
- **The describer leaves mid-turn:** the turn ends early, the card in hand scores 0.
- **The describer drops before starting:** someone else on the team takes the turn.
- **The describer drops during a buzz:** it goes straight to the captain.
- **The captain drops mid-dispute:** the next team member in seat order takes the call. If there's nobody, the buzz is dropped.
- **A team falls below 2 players:** the game ends. The higher score still wins; a tie has no winner.
- **The deck runs out:** it reshuffles, and the screen warns "Cards may repeat".
- **Host skip:** the host can skip someone the game is waiting on (the describer or the captain).
- **Play again:** keeps last game's teams for whoever's still here, with newcomers filling the smaller side. Cards from the last game are dealt last.

## Tips
- Guessers can talk as much as they like. Describers can't say the word, its forbidden words, or spell any of them out.
- A skip costs nothing, an Oops costs a point. If you're stuck, skip.
- Buzzing is a gamble. A dropped buzz costs nothing, but an upheld one gives the describer's team -1.
