# Liar's Dice

Everyone rolls in secret, then bids on what's under all the cups. Raise the bid or call the last bidder a liar. Whoever's wrong loses a die. 2–8 players.

## The short version

- Everyone has a cup of dice. Each round, everyone rolls, and you only see your own.
- Take turns bidding on the whole table: "four 3s" means at least four dice, across every cup, show a 3.
- On your turn, raise the bid or call **Liar!** on it.
- The cups come up. If the bid was there, the caller loses a die. If it wasn't, the bidder does. Last one with dice wins.

## How it plays

1. Everyone starts with five dice (or fewer, see Settings). The server rolls them all; each player sees only their own.
2. The player after the dealer opens with a bid: a quantity and a face, like "two 4s".
3. Play goes clockwise. On your turn, either:
   - **Raise.** More dice of any face, or the same number of a higher face. "four 3s" can go to "four 5s" or "five 2s".
   - **Liar!** You think there are fewer than that.
   - **Spot on.** You think there are exactly that many.
4. After a call every die is shown. The ones that count light up, the total counts up against the bid, and the verdict lands.
5. **Liar:** if the count is at least the bid, the caller loses a die. Otherwise the bidder does.
6. **Spot on:** exact, and the caller gets a die back (never above the starting number). Anything else, and the caller loses one.
7. The reveal stays up for about seven seconds, or until everyone still in taps **Next round**. Then everyone re-rolls.
8. Whoever lost a die opens the next round. If that took their last die, the next player still in opens. After a spot-on hit, the caller opens.

**Wild ones** (on by default): 1s count as every face. So "five 3s" is five dice showing a 3 or a 1. Bidding on 1s works differently:

- Switching **to** 1s takes at least half the quantity, rounded up. "seven 4s" can go to "four 1s".
- Switching **from** 1s takes at least double plus one. "four 1s" goes to "nine 4s" or more.
- Same quantity, higher face never moves you onto or off 1s.
- A bid **on** 1s counts only the 1s.

## Winning and scoring

Run out of dice and you're out. The last player with dice wins. The results list the winner with their dice left, then everyone else, latest out first ("out 3rd", "out 2nd", "out 1st"). Anyone the host removed comes last.

## Settings

Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wild ones | **On**, Off | On: 1s count as any face, with the half/double rule for bidding on them. Off: 1 is just the lowest face. |
| Spot on | **On**, Off | Allows the exact-count call. Off leaves raise or Liar. |
| Starting dice | 3, 4, **5** | Fewer dice, shorter game. Spot on never takes anyone above this. The lobby shows the total on the table. |

## On your screen

- **The others.** A row of players, each with a little face-down die per die they hold. The bidder is highlighted.
- **The table.** How many dice are in play, whether 1s are wild, the standing bid in big type (a number and a die), and this round's earlier bids.
- **Your cup.** Your own dice at the bottom. Nobody else sees them until a call.
- **Your turn.** A stepper for the quantity, six face buttons, and **Liar!**, **Spot on** and **Bid**. It starts on the smallest legal raise, so a nervous bid is one tap. Faces that can't beat the bid are greyed out, and the stepper won't go below what's legal.
- **The reveal.** Every cup turns over. Dice that count light up green; wild 1s get an outline. The count climbs against the bid, then the verdict ("Liar!", "It's there", "Spot on!", "Not quite") and who pays. The lost die is crossed out. A bar drains until the next round.
- Sounds and vibration cue the roll, bids, calls, your turn and the ending.

## When things go sideways

- **Disconnected player.** The game waits on their turn, and their chip shows them as reconnecting. The reveal doesn't wait for their tap.
- **Idle player.** The host gets a "Skip" button after a while. A skip bids for them, gently: one of the face they hold most of to open, otherwise the smallest raise on that face, otherwise the smallest raise at all. It never calls. (The one exception: a bid nothing can beat, where calling Liar is the only move left.)
- **The host removes a player.** Their dice leave the table. Mid-round, the round is void: everyone re-rolls and the next player after them opens. During a reveal, the result stands. If only one player has dice left, they win.
- **Game over.** The host sees Rematch and Back to lobby; everyone else sees Play again.

## Tips

- With wild ones on, a die you can't see shows any given face (or a 1) a third of the time. Twelve hidden dice: expect about four of a face, plus whatever's in your own cup.
- A bid on 1s is half as big but twice as rare. It's a cheap way to jump the table.
- Spot on is a long shot, but it's the only way to win a die back.
