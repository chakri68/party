# President

Shed your hand in singles, pairs and up, beating the pile or passing. First out is President; last out is the Asshole, and pays for it next round. 3–8 players.

## The short version

- The whole deck is dealt. Card order, low to high: 3 up to K, then A, then 2 on top.
- Lead a single, a pair, three or four of a kind. Everyone after you plays the same number of cards, of a higher rank, or passes.
- When everyone else has passed, the pile clears and the last player to play leads.
- First out is the President, last is the Asshole. Each round you score a point for every player you finished ahead of. Most points after the last round wins.

## How it plays

1. The deck is dealt out, clockwise from the dealer's left. Some hands are a card bigger.
2. In round 1, whoever holds the 3 of clubs leads. In later rounds the Asshole deals and leads (after the exchange below).
3. The leader plays one to four cards of one rank. This sets the size of the trick.
4. Going round, each player plays the same number of cards of a higher rank, or passes. If nothing in your hand can beat it, you're passed automatically ("Can't beat").
5. Once you've passed, you're out of that trick until it clears.
6. When everyone else has passed, the pile clears. Whoever played last leads anything. If they just went out, the lead goes to the next player on.
7. Go out by playing your last card. Your place decides your title for the next round.
8. When one player is left holding cards, they're last. The round is scored, and the next round is dealt straight away.

### Titles and the exchange

Titles come from where you finished. Vice titles only exist with four or more players; with three, it's President, Citizen, Asshole.

| Finished | Title |
| --- | --- |
| First | President |
| Second (4+ players) | Vice-President |
| In between | Citizen |
| Second-last (4+ players) | Vice-Asshole |
| Last | Asshole |

From round 2, before play starts:

1. The Asshole's two best cards go to the President, automatically.
2. From four players, the Vice-Asshole's best card goes to the Vice-President.
3. The President (and Vice-President) pick cards from their hand to give back, as many as they received. The button reads "Pick N to give", then "Give ...".
4. Play starts once all returns are in. The Asshole leads.

## Winning and scoring

You score one point per player you finished ahead of, so with 5 players first gets 4 points and last gets 0. Most points after the last round wins. A tie goes to whoever finished higher in the final round.

## Settings

Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rounds | 1, 2, **3**, 4, 5 | How many rounds are played. The card exchange only happens with more than one round. |
| Matching | **Must beat it**, Match to skip | Must beat it: only a higher rank goes on top. Match to skip: you may also play the same rank, and the next player is skipped. |

## On your screen

- **The pile.** The set to beat, and who played it. When you lead, it says "Your lead".
- **Other players.** Card count, points, title tag from last round, and a "D" for the dealer.
- **Round line.** "Round 2 of 3", plus "match to skip" if that's on.
- **Your hand.** Sorted from 3 up to 2. Tap cards to pick a set; the **Play** button names the set, and is disabled with a reason if the pick doesn't beat the pile.
- **Pass.** Only shown when there's something to pass on. You can't pass when leading.
- **Recap.** Between rounds, a list of the finish order and titles.
- Bubbles show "Can't beat", "Pass", "Skipped" and titles as people go out. Sounds and vibration cue the deal, plays, swaps, your turn and the ending.

## When things go sideways

- **Idle or disconnected player.** The host gets a "Skip" button after a while. On a trick, a skip is a pass. On a lead, it hands the lead to the next player. In the exchange, it gives back the player's lowest cards.
- **The host removes a player.** Their cards go out of play. Any swap they were part of is called off. If fewer than two players remain, the game ends. Round scoring counts only players who finished the round.
- **Game over.** The host sees Rematch and Back to lobby; everyone else sees Play again.

## Tips

- With Match to skip on, a pair of the same rank as the top set skips the next player.
- Finishing last costs you your two best cards next round, so the Asshole's cost goes beyond zero points.
