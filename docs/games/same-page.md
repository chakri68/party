# Same Page

Everyone answers the same prompt in secret. Score for matching the room, not for being clever. 3–12 players.

## The short version

- A prompt goes up: "The best biscuit", "A number from 1 to 10", "Name a fruit".
- Everyone types one answer before the timer runs out.
- Matching answers are grouped. The host can merge groups the machine missed.
- Everyone in the biggest group scores a point. Original thinking is a liability.

## How it plays

1. A prompt appears for everyone, with a draining bar for the answer time.
2. Type an answer and press **Lock in** (or Enter). You see "Locked in: pizza, tap to change", and can change it until time's up. Everyone sees who's answered (ticks, never the text).
3. Once everyone's in, the clock drops to 5 seconds, so a change of heart still fits but nobody waits out the rest.
4. Answers are grouped automatically (see Matching below). If there are at least two groups, the host gets about 20 seconds to tidy them up. Everyone can see the groups from here; the host's moves show live.
5. **Tidying (host only).** Tap one group, then the group it belongs with: "telly" goes into "tv". A group with something merged into it gets a **Split** button that undoes those merges. **Done, reveal** ends tidying early.
6. **Reveal.** Groups go up biggest first, with names under each and the winning group highlighted. Points for the round show next to names. After 8 seconds, or once everyone taps **Next prompt**, the next round starts.

### Matching

- Case, spaces, punctuation and accents don't matter: "Ice-cream" and "ice cream" match.
- A leading "a", "an" or "the" is dropped: "The Moon" matches "moon".
- Plurals match singulars: "cats" / "cat", "boxes" / "box", "berries" / "berry". Nothing gets cut below three letters.
- One-letter typos match on answers of five letters or more: "pepperoni" and "peperoni" join; "cat" and "car" don't. These typo matches come up already merged, so the host can **Split** them when the machine got it wrong ("horse" isn't "house").
- Matches chain: if A matches B and B matches C, all three are one group.
- A group's label is its most common spelling.
- Anything else ("7" and "seven", "telly" and "tv") is the host's call.

## Winning and scoring

- Everyone in the biggest group scores 1. If groups tie for biggest, they all score.
- A group of one never scores, even if every group is a group of one. Blank answers score nothing.
- **Lone answer** (setting): if exactly one player is alone with their answer, they lose a point. Never below 0. Two or more loners cost nothing.
- After the last prompt, the most points wins. Ties share it.

## Settings

Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rounds | 8, **12**, 16 | Prompts per game. The lobby shows a rough length. |
| Answer time | 20 s, **30 s**, 45 s | How long to type. Drops to 5 s once everyone's in. |
| Lone answer | **Off**, Costs a point | The only lone answer in a round loses a point. |

Prompts never repeat within a game, and a rematch draws ones the last game didn't use. There are a few hundred: Same Page's own, plus Face-Off's categories as "Name …".

## On your screen

- **Top.** Round number and seconds left, with a draining bar under them. It turns red in the last 5 seconds of answering, with a tick if you haven't answered.
- **The prompt.** Big, in a lime-edged card.
- **Answer box.** Lock in with the button or Enter. Locked in, it becomes a card showing your answer; tap it to change. Typed something and forgot to press Enter? It goes in by itself just before time's up.
- **Phones.** While the keyboard is up, the game shrinks to the clock, the prompt and the box, all above the keyboard. Scores and ticks come back when it closes.
- **Ticks.** Who's answered so far, and a count ("4 of 7 in").
- **Groups.** During tidying and the reveal: the label, how many, names, and what each person typed when it differs from the label.
- **Scoreboard.** Everyone's total, leader in lime, with this round's +1 or −1 at the reveal.
- Sounds and vibration cue a new prompt, locking in, the tidy-up, the reveal and the ending.

## When things go sideways

- **Disconnected player.** Nobody waits for them. An answer they'd already locked in still counts; otherwise their round is blank. Back before the timer ends, they can still answer.
- **Host drops during tidying.** The timer runs out and the automatic groups stand.
- **Idle players.** Rounds run on timers, so nobody blocks anyone. A host Skip during tidying goes straight to the reveal.
- **The host removes a player.** Their answer leaves the round and the groups are rebuilt; merges that lost a side are undone. Their score leaves the scoreboard. Under three players left, the game ends.
- **Answer refused.** Too long (over 40 characters) or too late: the box comes back with your text in it.
- **Game over.** The host sees Rematch and Back to lobby; everyone else sees Play again.

## Tips

- Don't answer what you think. Answer what you think everyone else thinks.
- "A number from 1 to 10" is rarely 1 or 10.
- Hosts: merge generously. Arguing about whether "biscuit" is "cookie" is half the fun, and it's out loud anyway.
