# Face-Off

Two of you are called out. Name something from the other's card, out loud, and buzz first. 3–12 players.

## The short version
- Everyone holds a category, face up: "a breakfast food", "something with wheels".
- Every few seconds two players are called out. Each says, out loud, something that fits the other's category.
- Said one? Buzz. The first buzz claims the point, unless the table overturns it.
- First to the target score wins. After 5 minutes the highest score wins.

## How it plays
1. **The quiet.** Between duels there's a pause of 4 to 9 seconds. You'll see "Wait for the spark". Know your card.
2. **The spark.** Two players are called out. Everyone sees who. Each of the two sees "Say out loud" with the category of the other's card, and what the other has to name from theirs.
3. **The duel (8 seconds).** Both of you say something from the other's category, out loud. When you've said one, tap **Buzz** (Enter or Space works on a keyboard). The first buzz claims the point.
4. **The call (6 seconds).** Everyone not in the duel sees two buttons: **Confirm: <the claimer>** and **Overturn: <the other duelist>**. The duelists can't vote.
   - More overturns than confirms: the point goes to the other duelist.
   - Otherwise, including ties and no votes at all, the claim stands.
   It closes early once every connected voter has voted.
5. **After.** The duelist who lost the point draws a new card. Then the quiet starts again.

## Redo
Either duelist can tap **Redo (no score)** during the duel or the call, as long as the duel isn't already a redo. No score, nobody draws, and the same two are called out again after a 2-second beat. A redo can't be redone.

## Winning and scoring
- Each duel is worth 1 point to whoever wins it.
- The game ends when someone reaches the target, or when the 5-minute clock falls.
- If the 5 minutes end during a duel, that duel gets to finish first. The game then ends, with the highest score winning.
- Ties for the top score share the win. If nobody scored, nobody wins.

## Settings
Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First to | **10 points**, 15 points, 20 points | The score that ends the game early. The 5-minute cap applies regardless. |

## On your screen
- **Your card:** your own category and score. It's face up to everyone else too.
- **The table:** every player with their score and category. Duelists are highlighted, and the leader is marked.
- **Header:** "First to 10", and a countdown such as "3:20 left", which changes to "Last duel" when the clock has fallen.
- **During a duel:** if you're in it, "Say out loud", the category you're naming from, and your own category as a reminder of what the other player has to name. The **Buzz** button sits underneath. Everyone else sees who's naming from whose card.
- **During the call:** the Confirm and Overturn buttons, and a count of how many have voted. You see how many, never how.
- **After a duel:** the result: "<name> +1", "Overturned", "Nobody buzzed", or "Duel off", with the vote count and who draws a new card.
- **Sounds:** a chime and a buzz when you are called out (a softer nudge for everyone else), a bang and a buzz on a buzz, a shuffle on a redo, and a ding when you win a duel.

## When things go sideways
- **Nobody buzzes in 8 seconds:** nobody scores and both duelists draw new cards.
- **A duelist leaves:** the duel is called off with no score, and nobody draws.
- **A duelist drops while still in the game:** duels only pick players who are connected, so they're skipped until they're back. The host can skip a duelist who's holding things up (the duel is voided, both draw).
- **A voter drops:** they stop holding the call open. If nobody is left to vote, the claim stands.
- **Fewer than 3 players left:** the game ends. Highest score wins.
- **The deck runs out:** it reshuffles, and the screen says so. Cards currently in hands aren't dealt twice.
- **Play again:** starts a new game. Categories from the last game are dealt last.

## Tips
- You won't be called out often, and the same pair doesn't come up twice in a row. A player who just dueled is also less likely to be picked again.
- Learn your own card well, and read the table. You can see everyone else's.
- If you're sure the other player buzzed with a miss, vote Overturn. The table is the only check.
