# The Wilds

One strange island and a team of explorers. Plan together, find 3 key stones, and all stand at the gate before the sun goes down. 2–6 players.

## The short version

- You all win or lose together. Nobody plays against anyone.
- Find a **key stone** at each of 3 old ruins. With all 3, open the **gate** in the middle of the island.
- Then every explorer must stand on the gate or next to it, and nobody can be down. This must happen before the sun goes down.
- Each round, everyone plans **2 actions** at the same time. Then the game plays them out, one after another.
- You can't see each other's plans. Talk out loud.
- The island has its own rules. Fire spreads through forest. Noise pulls the spider. Fire scares the spider. Most of the fun is in how these rules meet your plans.

## How it plays

1. **Roles (30 s, once).** Pick a role. Two players can pick the same role. Then each player gets 1 random item, and at least one player gets a Torch. The game starts when everyone has picked, or after 30 seconds. If you did not pick, you get a random role.
2. **Plan (45 s by default).** Look at this round's event at the top. Pick 2 actions, one for Beat 1 and one for Beat 2. Then tap **Ready**. You can tap **Edit** to change your plan until the time is up. The round goes on when every explorer who is not down is Ready, or when time runs out. If you made no plan, you Guard twice. An empty beat becomes Guard.
3. **The beats.** Beat 1 plays, then Beat 2. In each beat, every Help happens first. Then the other actions happen one player at a time. The order moves on by one seat each round. Each action is checked at the moment it plays, on the board as it is then. So a bridge fixed early in a beat can be crossed later in the same beat. An action that can no longer happen becomes Guard, and the report says why.
4. **Choice (20 s, some rounds).** Only when the event has options. Everyone votes, also players who are down. See Events.
5. **The island turn.** In this order:
   1. The event happens.
   2. Fire spreads, then burns down.
   3. The spider moves.
   4. Anyone standing in fire loses 1 Heart. Guard stops it.
   5. All noise goes down by 1.
   6. Win check. If the gate is open and everyone is at the gate and up, you win.
   7. The sun moves on by 1. If it reaches the limit, you lose.
   8. If every explorer is down, you lose.
6. **Report (8 s).** The screen plays the round back: moves, dice, fire and the spider. Below it are 1 to 4 plain lines about what happened. Tap **Next**, or wait 8 seconds.

## Winning and losing

- **Win:** at the win check, the gate is open, and every explorer is on the gate or next to it, and nobody is down. Bring down players back up first. Nobody gets left behind. The win check comes before the sun moves, so a win on the last round counts.
- **Lose:** the sun reaches the limit (10 by default), or every explorer is down at the end of a round.

The result says how it ended, for example "You escaped with 3 rounds left." or "The sun went down. 2 of 3 key stones found." Each player's Hearts are shown, or "Down". If you win, everyone on the team wins. If you lose, nobody wins.

Key stones belong to the team. Nobody carries them. Losing Hearts never loses them.

## The island

The island is a board of 37 hex tiles. There are 4 hand-made islands. Each one can be turned 6 ways, so you can meet 24 different boards. Water is all around, so nobody can leave the board.

| Tile | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Grass | You can walk here. |
| Forest | You can walk here. It can burn. |
| Reeds | You can walk here, but a Move stops here. |
| River | You can't walk here. Cross only on a bridge. |
| Bridge | You can walk here. It can burn or break. |
| Broken bridge | Like a river, until someone fixes it. |
| Rock | You can't walk here. |
| Camp, Gate, Ruin | You can walk here. Camp is where you start. |
| Ash | You can walk here. Burnt forest becomes ash. Ash can't burn again. |

Every island has a camp, a gate in the middle, 3 ruins, a spider nest next to one ruin, 3 caches and at least 2 bridges. Every ruin and the gate can be reached from the camp, even if any one bridge is broken.

**Fog.** The team shares one view. If one explorer sees a tile, everyone sees it. At the start, only the camp and the tiles next to it are open. The gate and the 3 ruins show as icons, so you know where to go, but you can't see the land around them. A tile opens when an explorer ends a Move next to it, or when a Look reaches it. Fire in the fog shows as smoke at the edge of the fog.

**Noise.** Each tile has noise from 0 to 3, shown as small marks. Noise comes from Bold tries, failed tries at ruins, Force, the Lure and some events. Noise goes down by 1 each island turn.

## Explorers

Each explorer has 3 **Hearts**, 2 actions each round and 2 bag slots for items. Each role has three scores: Strength, Mind and Stealth. A score helps your rolls at ruins.

| Role | Strength | Mind | Stealth | Power |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Runner | 0 | +1 | +2 | Your Move goes up to 3 tiles, not 2. |
| Fixer | +1 | +2 | 0 | Act on a broken bridge next to you to fix it. No item needed. |
| Protector | +2 | 0 | +1 | Your Guard also protects everyone on your tile. |
| Guide | 0 | +2 | +1 | You see next round's event one round early, only on your phone. Your Look reaches 3 tiles, not 2. |

No role is needed to win. Every ruin has a way in for every role, and a bridge can be fixed with Planks or walked around.

**Down.** At 0 Hearts you are **down**. You can't plan actions, but you still vote on choices. A friend can use **Help** on you to bring you back up with 1 Heart. The spider leaves down players alone. Fire can't take you below 0. You keep your items. Hearts never go above 3. If you are brought back up in a round, you Guard in that round. If you go down in a round, you do nothing more in that round.

## Actions

Each action uses 1 of your 2 actions.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Move | Walk up to 2 tiles (Runner: 3) on open land. The game finds the path. Reeds stop you. You can't walk into fire, the spider's tile or fog. The tiles next to where you stop open up. |
| Look | Open every tile within 2 tiles of you (Guide: 3). This shows caches and the spider. |
| Act | On your tile or a tile next to you. Try a ruin, open a cache, fix a bridge (Fixer only), or open the gate. To open the gate you need all 3 key stones and you must stand on the gate. |
| Item | Use an item from your bag. See Items. |
| Help | Pick a friend on your tile or next to you. If they are down, they get up with 1 Heart. If not, they get +1 on their roll in this beat. |
| Guard | Block the next 1 damage to you this round. Guard twice still blocks only 1. |

### Help, exactly

- Help works only if you are on your friend's tile or next to it at the start of the beat.
- Help happens first in each beat, before the other actions.
- If two friends help the same roll, it still gives only +1.
- If your friend does not roll in that beat, your Help does nothing. The report says so.
- You can't see plans, so Help is a promise you make out loud: "I will help you at the Shrine in Beat 2."

### Items

Each explorer has 2 bag slots. A full bag can't take an item from a cache. The Act fails, and the cache stays.

| Item | Use on | What it does | Used up? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Torch | A tile next to you | The spider there runs 3 tiles away from you, and does nothing next round. Forest or a bridge there starts to burn. Both at once is fine. | No |
| Planks | A broken bridge or a river tile next to you | It becomes a bridge. This bridge can burn too. | Yes |
| Medkit | You, or a friend next to you | +2 Hearts (never above 3). It does not bring a down player back. Use Help for that. | Yes |
| Lure | An open tile up to 3 tiles away | Set that tile's noise to 3. | Yes |

### Caches

Each island has 3 caches, hidden in fog. When you Act on an open cache, you draw 1 item: Planks, Medkit, Lure or Torch (weights 3, 3, 2 and 1). Then the cache is empty.

## Ruins and rolls

Each ruin has 1 key stone. Dice are rolled only at ruins. Moving, looking, items and helping never roll.

The roll is: **2 dice + your score + your way + Help (+1)**. If it is as high as the target number, you pass.

Each ruin has two **ways in**. Each uses one score and has a target of Easy (7) or Hard (9).

| Ruin | Way in | Score | Target | If you fail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sunken Shrine | Read the old words | Mind | Hard (9) | 2 noise on the ruin |
| | Lift the stone door | Strength | Easy (7) | 1 damage to you |
| Star Tower | Climb in quietly | Stealth | Hard (9) | 1 damage to you |
| | Turn the star wheel | Mind | Easy (7) | 3 noise on the ruin |
| Root Cave (next to the spider nest) | Sneak past the roots | Stealth | Easy (7) | 2 noise on the ruin |
| | Pull the roots away | Strength | Hard (9) | 1 damage to you |

**Way of acting.** You pick one when you plan an Act on a ruin:

| Way | Bonus | What else |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Careful | −1 | If you fail, the cost is smaller: just 1 noise on the ruin. |
| Normal | 0 | |
| Bold | +1 | 1 noise on your tile, whether you pass or fail. |

**Pass:** the team takes the key stone.

**Fail:** you pay the cost. The ruin is now **half open**. The next Act on it, by anyone, takes the key stone with no roll.

**Force:** you can choose Force instead of a way in. There is no roll. The team takes the key stone, but the sun moves on by 1 and the ruin gets 3 noise.

So a ruin takes 1 or 2 Acts. The roll decides how fast and how costly, but never whether you can get in. If someone else takes the key stone in the same beat, your Act becomes Guard.

You must stand on the ruin or next to it, and the ruin must be open (not in fog).

The chance to pass, by your total bonus (score + way + Help):

| Bonus | -1 | 0 | +1 | +2 | +3 | +4 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Easy (7) | 42% | 58% | 72% | 83% | 92% | 97% |
| Hard (9) | 17% | 28% | 42% | 58% | 72% | 83% |

The plan screen shows the chance before you tap Ready, for example "Mind +2 · Bold +1 · need 9 · 72%". It can't know about Help ahead of time, so it says "+1 if someone helps".

## Fire

- A tile can burn if it is forest or a bridge.
- A tile that catches fire burns until the end of the next round. Then forest becomes ash. A bridge becomes a broken bridge. A fire that comes from spreading lasts one round longer than a fire lit with a Torch, because it does not burn down in the round it starts.
- At the end of each round, each burning tile lights one forest or bridge tile next to it, picked at random. New fires do not spread in the round they start.
- At most 5 tiles can burn at once. Extra spreads do not happen.
- You can't walk into fire. If you stand on a tile that burns at the end of the round, you lose 1 Heart. Guard stops it.

## The spider

There is one spider. It starts on its nest, hidden in fog. Each island turn it does the first of these that fits:

1. **Scared.** If a Torch hit it this round, or if fire is on a tile next to it, it runs 3 tiles away from the fire or the Torch. The next round, it does nothing.
2. **Noise.** If a tile with noise is within 4 tiles, it moves up to 2 tiles toward the loudest one. A tie goes to the nearest, then to a fixed tile order.
3. **Hunt.** If an explorer who is not down is within 3 tiles, it moves up to 2 tiles toward the nearest one.
4. **Home.** It moves 1 tile toward its nest.

- It walks like an explorer: not over river, rock, fire or a broken bridge. Reeds do not stop it.
- It stops as soon as it is next to an explorer. If it ends its move on a tile next to an explorer, each explorer next to it loses 1 Heart. Guard stops it. It never steps onto an explorer.
- It leaves down players alone.
- The plan screen shows an arrow with where the spider goes **if nothing changes**. Your actions can change this. In fog, the spider and its arrow are hidden.

## Events

One event each round. You see it at the top of the plan screen, and it happens in step 1 of the island turn. Marked tiles have a dashed line on the board.

- Rounds 1 and 2 have only calm events.
- After a harm event, the next one is not harm.
- Only one bargain can be waiting at a time.
- An event that can't apply right now is skipped.
- The Guide sees the next event, on the event card, one round early.

| Event | Kind | What it does | Choice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet morning | Calm | Nothing happens. The spider stays still this round. | No |
| Old camp | Calm | The land around one hidden cache opens. | No |
| Bird calls | Calm | 3 hidden tiles next to open land open. | No |
| Clear sky | Calm | Open the land around the gate, or around the nearest ruin without its key stone. | Vote |
| Rain | Calm | All fire goes out. | No |
| Dry wind | Harm | Fire spreads two times this round. | No |
| Hungry spider | Harm | The spider moves 3 tiles this round, not 2. | No |
| Falling rocks | Harm | Anyone on the marked tile loses 1 Heart. Guard stops it. | No |
| Rotten bridge | Harm | The marked bridge breaks, unless an explorer stands on it at the end of the round. | No |
| Echo in the reeds | Harm | 2 noise on the marked reeds tile. | No |
| The stone hand | Bargain | **Accept:** every broken bridge is fixed now. In 2 rounds, the marked bridge gets 3 noise. **Refuse:** nothing happens. | Vote |
| The warm spring | Bargain | **Accept:** everyone gets 1 Heart, and down players get up with 1 Heart. The sun moves on by 1. **Refuse:** nothing happens. | Vote |

**Choice.** Everyone votes, also players who are down. Most votes wins. You can change your vote until time runs out. For a **bargain**, Accept needs more votes than Refuse. A tie, or no votes, means Refuse. For Clear sky, a tie goes to the first option.

**A waiting bargain.** If you accept the stone hand, a small bar shows "The stone hand: noise on this bridge in 2 rounds." The marked bridge has a mark on the board. The noise always happens.

## Settings

Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sun limit | 8 (hard), **10**, 12 (easy) | How many rounds you have. The sun goes down after this many rounds. |
| Plan time | 30 s, **45 s** (default), 60 s | Time to plan each round. |

Other timers are fixed: 30 s to pick a role, 20 s to vote on a choice, 8 s for the round report. A game takes about 12 to 18 minutes.

## On your screen

- **The board.** 37 hex tiles. Each tile type has its own colour and a small icon, so colour is never the only sign. Fog is grey and has no detail. You see fire, noise marks, caches, marked event tiles, and a token for each explorer (the first letter of the name, with a ring for the role). The camp, the gate and the ruins are shown as icons, even in fog.
- **The top bar.** The sun ("Sun 4 / 10"), the 3 key stones, the round and the timer.
- **The spider's arrow.** On the plan screen, an arrow shows where the spider goes if nothing changes. A key under the board explains it: "Where the spider goes if nothing changes."
- **The event card.** This round's event, and what its marked tiles are. The Guide also sees "Only you see this: next round is ..." with the next event.
- **The team.** Each explorer, role, Hearts, and whether they are Ready or still planning. Down players show "Down". You see who has voted in a choice, but not how.
- **Roles.** At the start you see each role with its scores and power, and who picked it. Your starting item is shown with what it does.
- **The plan tray.** Two slots, Beat 1 and Beat 2, and 6 action cards. Tap a card, and the tiles you can use light up on the board. The others go dim. Tap a dim tile to see why you can't use it, for example "Fog: you can't plan a path there." or "River: you can't walk there. Find a bridge." For Help and Medkit, you can also tap a name. An Act on a ruin opens a small sheet with both ways in, the three ways of acting, their chances, the fail cost, and Force. When you tap **Ready**, the tray locks. Tap **Edit** to change it.
- **The pending bargain bar.** After you accept the stone hand, a bar above the board says when and where the noise will come.
- **The choice.** A vote screen with the options, how many have voted, and a note about ties.
- **The round report.** The board plays the round back step by step: moves, dice (with every bonus), fire and the spider. Tap **Skip to the end** to jump to the end. If your phone is set to reduce motion, the board shows only the end and the lines. After it, 1 to 4 plain lines say what happened, and who is down. Tap **Next** when you are ready.
- **Sounds and buzzes** mark a new plan and a choice.

At the end of the party, The Wilds counts toward the party awards. Everyone on a winning team counts as a winner. Each Help that gets a down friend up counts toward **Always there**. Each fire you light with a Torch counts toward **Fire starter**. Each Refuse vote on a bargain counts toward **Hardest to convince**.

## When things go wrong

- **You do not plan in time.** The plan you have at that time is used. If you have no plan, you Guard twice. An empty beat becomes Guard.
- **You do not pick a role in time.** You get a random role.
- **You do not vote in time.** Your vote is not counted. For a bargain, a tie becomes Refuse.
- **A move can't happen.** If something blocks it (fire, the spider, a broken bridge) by the time it plays, you Guard instead. The report says why.
- **Someone's phone dies, or the host skips them.** In a plan, they Guard and are Ready. In a choice, they do not vote. In roles, they get a random role.
- **Someone leaves the game.** Their explorer leaves the board and their items are lost. The win check does not count them.
- **Everyone leaves.** The game ends early.

## Tips

- Talk first. Say your plan out loud, but not too early: plans change when the fire or the spider moves.
- Check the spider's arrow before you walk. If it is going where you are going, use Guard or a Torch.
- A Torch can help and hurt. Fire scares the spider, but it also breaks bridges and blocks the way.
- A failed try is not a loss. The ruin becomes half open, and the next Act takes the stone. Careful costs less when you fail.
- Use Force when time is short. It costs 1 round of sun, but it needs no roll and no second try.
- Bold makes noise. Noise pulls the spider. Do not be Bold when the spider is near.
- Keep Help for rolls and for down friends. A down friend must be brought back before you can win.
- Keep your team close to the gate near the end, but not on a forest tile next to fire.
