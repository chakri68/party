# Heist

Five jobs, a crew you don't quite trust, and a mole or three who'd love to see it all go wrong. 5–10 players.

## The short version

- You're crew or a mole. Moles know each other. Crew know only themselves.
- There are five jobs. For each, the leader picks a crew, and everyone votes **Go** or **No-go**.
- If the vote passes, the crew plays cards face down. One Sabotage card fails the job.
- First side to three jobs wins.

## How it plays

1. **Your card (30 s).** Tap the card to see crew or mole. Moles also see the other moles' names and their Sabotage cards left. Tap **Got it**. The game starts when everyone has, or at 30 seconds. A random player leads first.
2. **Proposal.** The leader picks a crew of the job's size and taps **Propose**. The leader may pick themselves. The size is shown on the board. Everyone else is told "Make your case out loud."
3. **Vote.** The crew is shown. Everyone, on the crew or not, taps **Go** or **No-go**. More than half of the whole table must say Go. A tie fails. You can change your vote until the clock runs out or everyone's in. When it closes, everyone's votes are shown by name under "Last vote".
4. **On the job.** If it passed, each crew member plays one card, face down: **Clean** or **Sabotage**. Cards are counted when the clock runs out, not before. You can change yours until then.
5. **Result.** One Sabotage and the job fails. The table sees how many Sabotage cards went in, never whose. Then the next job.

The leader moves one seat on after every proposal, passed or not.

## Heat

Every failed vote adds one heat. At 3 heat the job is lost to the moles without being played. Heat resets to zero after every job, however it ended.

## Roles

Two cards. There's no deck to configure: the Moles setting just shifts the count.

### Crew

- **Team:** crew.
- **Does:** proposes crews when leading, votes, and plays a card on jobs. Sabotage is on the screen for you too, but it plays as Clean.
- **Learns:** nothing about anyone else. The card says "Pull off three jobs. Some of your crew are lying."

### Mole

- **Team:** moles.
- **Does:** the same, but a Mole's Sabotage counts. Each Mole has 2 Sabotage cards for the whole game. When they're spent, a Sabotage plays as Clean.
- **Learns:** who the other Moles are, and how many Sabotage cards they have left.

## Everyone looks the same on a job

On a job, every crew member sees the same two buttons, **Clean** ("Job goes ahead") and **Sabotage** ("Job fails"), the same headline, and the same line under them: "Cards stay face down. Nobody sees yours." The server takes a card as tapped from anyone. Crew Sabotage, or a Mole's with none left, is quietly played as Clean. Nobody's screen refuses a tap or shows a different result, so a phone can't tell who's a Mole. A Mole's count of cards left is on their card, behind the tap to reveal.

The job also never ends early when everyone has played: it always runs to the clock, so a fast finish tells nobody anything. Who's still voting or holding a card is never shown, either.

## Winning and scoring

First side to 3 jobs wins. A job goes to the crew if no Sabotage card counted. It goes to the moles on one or more Sabotage cards, or on 3 heat.

A game cut short by people leaving has no winner (see below).

## Settings

Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pace | **Beginner** (default), Standard, Fast | Timers. |
| Moles | **Usual** (default), One fewer, One more | How many Moles. |

Pace timers:

| Pace | Leader to propose | Vote | Cards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner | 60 s | 30 s | 20 s |
| Standard | 45 s | 20 s | 20 s |
| Fast | 30 s | 15 s | 10 s |

Moles: usual is 2 at 5–6 players, 3 at 7–8, 4 at 9–10. There are always at least 2 Moles and always fewer Moles than crew. In practice that means **One fewer** only appears from 7 players, and **One more** never fits any table size (it's only listed if already set, and then the lobby says why it can't start). With fewer than 5 players the options show no numbers.

## On your screen

- **The board.** Five job pips with their crew sizes, turned into a tick (came off), a cross (sabotaged) or a flame (lost to heat). Below, the score ("Crew 1 · Moles 2"), the heat dots and the mole count.
- **Your card.** Face down. Tap to reveal, tap to hide. It hides itself when you switch tabs or apps.
- **Leader.** On your lead you get the list of players; tap to pick and tap again to remove. Tap **Propose** when the count's right. "That's N. Tap someone to take them off."
- **Last vote and last job.** After the first one, the previous vote shows by name, with a note if someone missed it, and the last job shows its crew and how many Sabotage cards went in.
- **Debrief.** At the end: who was who, every job, every proposal with its votes. Never a card.
- **Sounds and buzzes** mark your turn to lead, the crew being proposed, vote results, job results, and the end.

## When things go sideways

- **The leader doesn't propose in time.** That's a failed vote: heat +1, the lead moves on.
- **You don't vote in time.** Your vote is No-go, flagged as "(missed)".
- **A crew member doesn't play a card in time.** Their card plays as Clean. The result notes how many were "auto-played as Clean", never whose.
- **Someone's connection drops.** They don't hold up the reveal or the vote. If nobody's connected, the clock decides.
- **A crew member leaves after the crew is proposed** (during the vote or the job). The proposal is voided: no heat, cards dropped unplayed, no Sabotage spent, and the lead goes back to whoever proposed.
- **The leader leaves while picking.** The next seat takes over with a fresh clock.
- **The game ends early with no winner** if fewer than 5 players are left ("Too few players left"), the last mole leaves ("The last mole left"), or no crew are left.
- **Rematch.** Non-hosts tap **Play again**; the host taps **Rematch** or **Back to lobby**. A new deal, a random leader.

## Tips

- The vote is the only real tool crew have. Everyone's vote is public afterwards, so it's worth reading.
- A Mole has only 2 Sabotage cards for the whole game. Spend one early and you have one left.
- Crew can pick themselves, which is the one crew member you know.
