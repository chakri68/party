# Farkle

Roll six dice, keep the scorers, push your luck or bank. Roll nothing that scores and the turn's points are gone. 2–8 players.

## The short version

- On your turn the six dice roll for you. Keep at least one scoring die, then roll the rest or bank.
- Points pile up in a turn total. Banking adds it to your score.
- Roll nothing that scores and it's a farkle: the turn total is gone.
- Bank to the target and everyone else gets one more turn. Highest score wins.

## How it plays

1. The first player is picked by seat and moves round the table from one game to the next. Turns go in seat order.
2. Your turn starts with all six dice rolled. Tap the dice you want to keep. Every die you keep has to score, and only dice from the same roll combine: a 5 you kept earlier doesn't make a triple with two 5s rolled later.
3. Press **Keep & roll N** to set them aside and roll what's left, or **Keep & bank X** to set them aside and add the turn total to your score.
4. Set aside all six and you've got **hot dice**: all six roll again and the turn total keeps growing.
5. A roll with nothing that scores is a **farkle**. The dead roll stays on the table for about two seconds, the turn total is struck out, and the dice pass on.
6. Until you're on the board, your first bank has to reach the entry threshold. Below it, you can only keep and roll.

## Winning and scoring

When someone banks to the target or past it, the final round starts: every other player gets exactly one more turn. Then the highest score wins. Ties share the win. Results show everyone's points.

A selection scores the best way it can be split into these:

| Combination | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Single 1 | 100 |
| Single 5 | 50 |
| Three 1s | 1,000 |
| Three of a kind, 2–6 | face × 100 |
| Four of a kind | 1,000 |
| Five of a kind | 2,000 |
| Six of a kind | 3,000 |
| Straight 1–6 | 1,500 |
| Three pairs (four of a kind plus a pair counts) | 1,500 |
| Two triples | 2,500 |

So `1 1 1 1 5 5` is 1,500 as three pairs, not 1,100 as four of a kind and two 5s. Four 1s are 1,100 (three 1s and a 1), not 1,000.

## Settings

Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target | **5,000**, 10,000 | Where the final round starts. 10,000 is the traditional length; 5,000 suits a party. |
| Entry threshold | Off, 350, **500** | Your first bank must be at least this much. |
| Three farkles | **Off**, −500 | Three farkles in a row cost 500. Scores never go below 0. A bank resets the count. |

## On your screen

- **Scoreboard.** Every player's score with a bar toward the target. A dash means not on the board yet. The current player is highlighted. With three farkles on, red crosses count a player's farkles in a row.
- **Final round banner.** Who hit the target, with a star on their chip, and who still has a turn to go.
- **Turn total.** Above the dice. Struck out in red on a farkle.
- **Set aside.** The dice kept this turn, one group per roll, each with its points.
- **The roll.** Tap dice to pick them; picked dice lift and go lime. A die that can't score goes red and the line under the dice says so ("That 3 doesn't score"). Otherwise it shows what you're keeping and what the turn would come to.
- **Watching.** Everyone sees the same table, including the roller's picks as they make them.
- **Buttons.** "Keep & roll N" and "Keep & bank X". Bank is greyed out below the entry threshold, with a note saying so.
- Sounds and vibration cue the roll, keeps, banks, farkles, hot dice, the final round, your turn and the ending.

## When things go sideways

- **Idle player.** The host gets a "Skip" button after a while. A skip keeps the best-scoring dice and banks. If that's not enough to get on the board, it keeps them and rolls instead.
- **Disconnected player.** The game waits, and their chip shows them as reconnecting. The host can skip or remove them.
- **The host removes a player.** They're out of the rotation. If it was their turn, it ends with nothing banked. If they started the final round, it still runs. If only one player is left, they win.
- **Game over.** The host sees Rematch and Back to lobby; everyone else sees Play again.

## Tips

- Two dice left is roughly a coin flip to farkle. One die left is worse: only a 1 or a 5 saves you.
- You don't have to keep every scorer. Keeping a lone 1 and rolling five dice is often better than keeping 1, 5 and rolling four.
- In the final round, you know the number to beat. Bank short of it and you've lost anyway.
