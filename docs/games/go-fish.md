# Go Fish

Ask someone for a rank you hold. Collect all four to make a book. Most books wins. 2–6 players.

## The short version

- Ask another player for a rank you already have in your hand.
- If they have any, they hand over every card of that rank and you go again.
- If not, "Go fish": you draw one card from the pond. If it's the rank you asked for, you go again; otherwise the turn passes.
- Four of a rank make a book, and it goes down on its own. Most books when all 13 are down wins.

## How it plays

1. Each player gets 7 cards with two or three players, 5 with four or more. The rest go face down in the middle: the pond. Any book dealt to you goes straight down.
2. The player left of the dealer asks first.
3. On your turn, tap a rank in your hand, then tap the player you want to ask. The button then reads "Ask <name> for <ranks>". Press it. You can only ask for a rank you hold, and only a player who still has cards.
4. Everyone hears every ask and answer. If the other player has the rank, they hand over all of those cards, face up, and you go again.
5. If they don't, they say "Go fish." You draw the top card of the pond. What you draw is private, unless it's the rank you asked for: that one is shown, and you go again.
6. When you complete a rank, the four cards go down as a book.
7. If your hand runs empty, you draw 5 cards from the pond (or whatever's left) and carry on. If the pond is empty too, you sit out the rest of the game.

## Winning and scoring

The game ends when all 13 books are down. Most books wins, and ties share the win. If nobody has made a book, nobody wins. Standings list players by book count.

## On your screen

- **Other players.** Card count, books (hover for which ranks), a "D" for the dealer, and who's asking.
- **The table.** The pond, with its count ("Pond's empty" at zero), and "N of 13 books down".
- **Recent asks.** A short log of the last eight asks and what each got, so you don't have to remember.
- **Your hand.** Sorted by rank so a rank's cards sit together. Only ranks you hold can be asked for.
- **Status line.** "Your turn: ask someone", or "<name> is asking". When you're out of cards you see "Out of cards" and "Watching".
- Sounds and vibration cue the deal, hand-overs, books, your turn and the ending.

## When things go sideways

- **Empty pond.** If the player you ask doesn't have the rank and there's nothing left to draw, the ask finds nothing and your turn ends.
- **Idle or disconnected player.** The host gets a "Skip" button after a while. A skip just ends that player's turn. Handing cards over is automatic, so nobody else is held up.
- **The host removes a player.** Their hand is shuffled back into the pond, and their books stay down. They finish last, whatever they'd made. If anyone was sitting out on an empty pond, they draw back in. If only one player is left, the game ends.
- **Game over.** The host sees Rematch and Back to lobby; everyone else sees Play again.

## Tips

- Asking for a rank tells everyone you hold it. The recent-asks log is how you find out who's holding what.
