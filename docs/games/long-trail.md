# Long Trail

One caravan, a long trail and not enough food. Plan each day together, and keep your own goal secret. 3–8 players.

## The short version

- You all travel together in one caravan, from Dunmar to Mirren Gate. Everyone wins or loses the trip together.
- Each day one player leads. The leader plans the day, and everyone votes **Go** or **No-go**.
- Something happens on the trail every day. Some events give you a choice, and everyone picks.
- Buy goods where they are cheap. Sell them where they pay more. At the end, your coins and goods give the caravan a grade.
- Everyone has a job that the whole table can see. What your job tells you, and your goal, are only on your phone.

## How it plays

1. **Your job (30 s).** You see your job, what it tells you, and your secret goal. Tap **Got it**. The game starts when everyone has tapped, or after 30 seconds. A random player leads first.
2. **Plan (45 s).** The leader picks:
   - **Pace:** Slow (20 km a day), Steady (30 km) or Hard (40 km).
   - **Rations:** Full, Half or None.
   - **Rest today:** the caravan does not move.
   - **Which way?** at a fork.
   - **Medicine:** who gets a dose.
   - **Market:** what to buy and sell, at a town with a market.

   Then the leader taps **Propose**. Everyone else talks about it out loud.
3. **Vote (25 s).** Everyone taps **Go** or **No-go**. You can change your vote until the vote closes. More Go than No-go passes the plan. A tie fails. If the plan fails, the next player makes a second plan. If that fails too, the caravan keeps yesterday's pace and rations, and nothing else.
4. **The day.** Trades happen first, then medicine, then the caravan travels. It eats and drinks, and each traveller may get worse. Then an event happens.
5. **Choice (20 s).** Some events have 2 options. Everyone picks one. Most picks wins. A tie goes to the leader's pick, or to the first option if the leader did not pick.
6. **Day report (5 s).** How far you moved, what you lost or got, and who got sick. Tap **Next**. Then the next player leads.

The game ends when the caravan reaches Mirren Gate, when time runs out, or when every traveller is dead.

## The trail

The trail goes from stop to stop. The part between two stops is a **leg**. Each leg is plains, desert, mountains or river.

The trail has forks. At a fork, the leader must pick a way: no way is picked for you. One way starts with a leg that is never dangerous. The two ways are not the same length. Only the Scout sees how long each way is.

| Length | Stops on your way | Days you have |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short | 5 | 12 |
| Normal | 8 | 20 |
| Epic | 12 | 32 |

The top of the screen shows "Day 5 of 20". You must reach Mirren Gate by the last day.

## Food, water and health

You start with 14 food and 10 water for each player, 3 medicine, 420 coins, 2 of each good (silk, spice, salt), and 2 camels plus 1 for every 2 players (rounded up).

Each day, each living traveller eats 2 food on full rations, 1 on half, 0 on none. Each one drinks 1 water, or 2 in the desert. Rest days use food and water too.

Each traveller has health from 0 to 3: **Well** (3), **Sick** (2), **Badly hurt** (1), **Dead** (0).

Each day, things that are bad for health add risk points. Each risk point is a 20% chance to lose 1 health that day:

| What | Risk points |
| --- | --- |
| Hard pace | 1 |
| Half rations | 1 |
| No rations | 2 |
| Not enough water | 2 |
| Not enough food | 1 |
| Already below Well | 0.5 |

On a rest day with full rations, each traveller below Well has a 60% chance to get 1 health back. A dose of medicine adds 1 health. You can give medicine only on a rest day, unless the Healer is alive.

**Camels** get tired on Hard days. Each Hard day adds 1. Each rest day takes away 2. At 3, the next Hard day kills one camel, and the count goes back to 0.

Each camel carries 6 goods. If you carry more than that, the caravan walks one pace slower.

## Events

Each leg is calm, rough or dangerous. This is set at the start and is hidden. The Guide can see it for the next leg. A calm leg has mostly calm events. A dangerous leg has mostly bad ones. The first leg of the safer way at a fork is calm or rough, never dangerous.

Events can give or take food, water, coins, goods, camels, health and time. Lost time means the caravan is pushed back along the leg, but never past the last stop.

## Markets and trade

Some towns have a market. There, the leader can buy food, water, medicine and sometimes camels, and buy or sell silk, spice and salt. Prices are set at the start of the game and stay the same all game.

Each town pays a different price for each good. Mirren Gate, at the end, pays well for everything, but it is not always the best price. Some towns on the way can pay more for silk.

| Town | Silk | Spice | Salt | Medicine | Camel | On which length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tel Orrin | 15–18 | 15–18 | 17–22 | 9 | – | Epic |
| Saltmere | 10–18 | 18–28 | 8–13 | 8 | 22 | All |
| Vessa Pass | 8–14 | 16–24 | 14–20 | 9 | – | All |
| Kessel Wells | 18–28 | 9–17 | 9–15 | 9 | – | Normal, Epic |
| Rook Pass | 16–26 | 8–14 | 16–24 | – | – | Normal, Epic |
| Lorn Ferry | 19–23 | 19–23 | 16–19 | 8 | 23 | Epic |
| Gorrow Heights | 12–18 | 18–26 | 9–14 | 10 | – | Epic |
| Yarrin | 10–18 | 10–16 | 16–24 | 8 | 24 | Normal, Epic |
| Quillon | 18–30 | 11–18 | 10–16 | 8 | 22 | Short |
| Mirren Gate | 22–30 | 22–30 | 18–26 | 10 | 26 | All |

Food and water cost 1 coin each at every market. You can sell goods, but not food, water, medicine or camels. Trades in one plan sell first, then buy, so a sale can pay for a buy.

When the Trader leads, the caravan buys 10% cheaper and sells 10% higher.

## Jobs

Each player has one job. Everyone sees who has which job. What the job tells you is only on your phone. If your traveller dies, your job's help stops working. You still see what your job tells you, and you still lead and vote.

| Job | Sees | Help |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guide | How dangerous the next leg is. | Once per game, swaps an event that has no choice for a new one. |
| Healer | The chance that each sick traveller gets worse today. | Medicine can be given on a travel day, not only on a rest day. |
| Trader | The prices at the next market town. | Better prices on days the Trader leads. |
| Quartermaster | How many days of food and water are left. | Once per game, finds 10 food. |
| Scout | How long each way is at the next fork. | Once per game, cancels the time lost to an event. |
| Driver | How tired the camels are. | Once per leg, a Hard pace adds no health risk. |
| Cook | How long the food lasts on full and half rations. | One day per leg, half rations count as full for health. |
| Lookout | The chance that each option in a choice goes well. | Once per game, swaps a choice for a different one. |

**Get ready.** The Guide, Scout, Quartermaster and Lookout tap **Get ready** during a plan or a vote. The help works by itself the next time it can, and is then used up:

- Guide: on the next event that has no choice, good or bad.
- Lookout: on the next choice.
- Scout: on the next event that costs time.
- Quartermaster: on the next day the caravan moves.

Tap **Cancel** to stop it before then. The day report says when a help was used.

## Secret goals

Each player gets one secret goal. Only you see yours, until the end.

- Arrive with at least 250 coins. Or 400 coins.
- Make sure one named traveller reaches the end alive. It also counts if that player leaves the game.
- Arrive a few days early: by day 11 (Short), 18 (Normal) or 29 (Epic).
- Arrive many days early: by day 10 (Short), 16 (Normal) or 26 (Epic).

Your goal card shows the exact day.
- Visit a town on one side of a fork.
- Arrive with at least 10 silk, 10 spice or 10 salt.
- Never set rations to None, not even for one day.
- Nobody dies.
- Arrive with a Fair grade or better. Or a Rich grade or better.

Goals about coins, goods, grade and arriving early need the caravan to arrive. Coin goals count only coins, not goods.

## Winning and scoring

At Mirren Gate, the caravan sells all its goods at Mirren Gate's prices. Coins plus that sale is the caravan's wealth. Wealth gives a grade:

| Grade | Wealth |
| --- | --- |
| Poor | under 585 |
| Fair | 585 |
| Rich | 852 |
| Very rich | 1250 |

Points for each player:

| What | Points |
| --- | --- |
| The caravan arrives | +5 |
| Grade | Poor 0, Fair +2, Rich +4, Very rich +6 |
| Your goal is met | +3 |
| Your traveller is alive at the end | +2 |

The most points wins. Ties share the win.

If the caravan does not arrive, nobody wins. Points are still shown, and so are the goals, so you can talk about whose fault it was.

## Settings

Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setting | **Trade route** | Camels, desert and markets. Only one for now. |
| Length | Short, **Normal** (default), Epic | Short: 5 stops, about 15 min. Normal: 8 stops, about 25 min. Epic: 12 stops, about 45 min. |

Timers are fixed: 30 s to read your job, 45 s to plan, 25 s to vote, 20 s to choose, 5 s for the day report, 20 s for the trip at the end.

## On your screen

- **The trail.** A line of stops, with the forks ahead. Below it, where you are, or how many km are left to the next stop.
- **Stock.** Food and water as bars: plenty (6 days or more), some (3 to 5), low (1 or 2), out. Medicine, coins, camels and each good as numbers, and your load out of what the camels can carry.
- **The caravan.** Each player, their job, their health, and who leads today.
- **You.** Your job, what it tells you, your help and its **Get ready** button, and your secret goal behind a tap.
- **The plan.** The leader gets the plan form. Everyone else sees the plan when it is proposed, then **Go** and **No-go**. While the vote is open, you see who has voted, not how. When it closes, everyone's vote is shown by name.
- **Day report.** After the day, and again under the next plan.
- **The trip.** At the end: the route with the day you reached each stop, who lived, the closest votes, the worst day, everyone's job and goal with Met or Missed, and the money and grade.
- **Sounds and buzzes** mark your turn to lead, a new plan, the vote result, a choice, the day report and the end.

At the end of the party, Long Trail counts toward the party awards. Each No-go vote counts toward "Hardest to convince".

## When things go wrong

- **The leader does not plan in time.** The caravan gets yesterday's pace and rations as the plan, and everyone votes on it. At a fork, it takes the safer way.
- **You do not vote in time.** Your vote is not counted.
- **You do not choose in time.** Your pick is not counted. If nobody picks, the first option is used.
- **Someone's connection drops.** They do not hold up the game. The clock decides.
- **Someone leaves.** Their traveller leaves the caravan. This is not a death. They get no score. If they were planning or being voted on, the next player plans fresh.
- **Only 1 player is left.** The game ends early with no winner.

## Tips

- Everyone can see your job, so the table knows what you know. Say it out loud, or keep quiet. Your goal may want something different.
- The end town is not always the best place to sell. The Trader sees the next market's prices first.
- Ask the Scout and the Guide before you pick a way.
- Half rations save a lot of food, but people get sick. Rest days with full rations bring them back.
- Watch the camels. A Hard pace for many days in a row costs you a camel, and a lost camel means less to carry.
