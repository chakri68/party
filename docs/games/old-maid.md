# Old Maid

Pair off your cards, take blind from your neighbour. Whoever's left holding the odd one loses. 2–10 players.

## The short version

- One card in the deck can never be paired: the Old Maid.
- Pairs (same rank, any suit) go down on their own, right after the deal and whenever you make one.
- On your turn, take one card, face down, from the player before you.
- Run out of cards and you're out, safe. The last player holding cards loses.

## How it plays

1. The whole deck is dealt, one at a time, clockwise from the dealer's left. Some players get one more card than others.
2. Everyone's pairs go down face up, automatically. Three of a kind is a pair plus a spare; four is two pairs.
3. The player left of the dealer goes first. You take one card from the player before you, picking from their face-down fan. When it's your turn the status says "Your turn: take one from <name>".
4. Pick a card in the fan. On a mouse, clicking takes it straight away; on touch, tap once to select, then tap again or press **Take it**. If the new card matches something in your hand, the pair goes down.
5. Your fan is shuffled whenever you receive a card, so the next taker can't tell where anything came from.
6. If you have no cards, you're out and safe. That includes having your last card taken. You see "You're out · 1st" (or 2nd, 3rd...), and you watch the rest.
7. Play goes round, skipping players who are out, until one player is left.

## Winning and scoring

The last player holding cards loses: they're "stuck with" the Old Maid. Everyone else wins. Standings show the order people got out, then the loser.

## Settings

Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Old Maid | **A queen (classic)**, The joker | Queen: the queen of clubs is removed (51 cards), so one of the other three queens ends up with no partner, and nobody knows which until the end. Joker: a joker is added (53 cards), and whoever holds it knows. The lobby shows the cards per player. |

## On your screen

- **The fan.** The cards you're taking from, face down. On your turn it's the player to your right; otherwise you can watch whose fan is being picked from.
- **Discard pile.** Shows how many pairs are down and the latest pair.
- **Other players.** Card count, a "D" for the dealer, and their finishing place once they're out.
- **Your hand.** Sorted by rank, always visible to you only. You'll see "You took ..." when you pick a card, and when a card is taken from you.
- **Take it.** On touch, appears once you've selected a card from the fan.
- With the joker, you get an "Uh oh" bubble when you take it and "Good riddance" when someone takes it from you. "Out!" marks anyone who goes out.
- Sounds and vibration cue the deal, takes, pairs, your turn and the ending.

## When things go sideways

- **Idle or disconnected player.** The host gets a "Skip" button after a while. A skip takes a random card for that player, so they don't sit out.
- **The host removes a player.** Their cards go to the next player still holding cards, who pairs off what they can. If this leaves fewer than two players holding cards, the game is called off ("Called off: too many people left") and nobody loses.
- **Game over.** The host sees Rematch and Back to lobby; everyone else sees Play again.

## Tips

- Taking the last card from someone gets them out, not you. You still have to keep taking.
- In the joker version, everyone knows the target, so watch who's holding on.
