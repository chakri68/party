# Overheat

Flip cards for points. Every card also adds heat. Go over the heat limit and you get nothing for the round. Save in time, and push cards onto other players. 2–8 players.

Computer players can fill seats in this game. They flip first and save when the heat limit is close. They aim a Push at the player closest to the limit, and a Burn at the leader.

## The short version

- Each round has a heat limit. Everyone can see it.
- On your turn, flip a card or save.
- A number card adds its number to your heat and to your points.
- If your heat goes over the limit, you are too hot. You get 0 points for the round.
- Save to keep your points. After you save, other players' cards can't hurt you.
- The first player to reach the target score at the end of a round wins.

## How it plays

1. At the start of each round the game picks a heat limit from 18 to 30. Everyone's heat starts at 0 and every engine (your row of cards) is empty.
2. Turns go round the table. The first player moves one seat each round.
3. On your turn, press **Flip** to turn over the top card, or **Save +N** to keep your points and leave the round. On your first turn of a round you must flip, so you can't save 0.
4. A number card goes into your engine. Its number goes on your heat and on your points.
5. If your heat goes over your limit, you are **too hot**. You are out of the round with 0 points. This is checked after every change, so a card from another player can make you too hot on their turn.
6. If your heat is exactly your limit, that's **Perfect**: +10 points, and you save at once.
7. The round ends when nobody is still flipping. Everyone's round result stays on screen for a few seconds, then the next round starts.
8. When the deck runs out, the used cards are shuffled into a new deck.

## Power cards

Power cards add no heat and no points of their own. With the **Classic** setting there are 16 in the deck of 88.

| Card | Copies | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cool | 4 | Your heat goes down by 5. Never below 0. |
| Double | 3 | Your next number card this round counts double: double heat, double points. |
| Push | 3 | Pick a player. The next card from the deck goes into their engine. If it's a power card, they play it. |
| Swap | 2 | Pick a player. Swap one of your number cards with one of theirs. Heat and points move with the cards. |
| Burn | 2 | Pick a player. At the start of each of their turns, their heat goes up by 1. A player can have more than one. |
| Limit down | 2 | The limit goes down by 3 for every player still flipping, you too. |

Push, Swap and Burn can only be aimed at players who are still flipping. If there is nobody to aim at, the card does nothing.

## Winning and scoring

When you save, you score:

- the numbers on your cards (a card played after Double counts double),
- +5 for each colour you have 3 or more cards of,
- +5 if you have all 4 colours,
- +10 for Perfect.

At the end of a round, if anyone has reached the target, the game ends. The highest score wins. Ties share the win. Results show everyone's points.

## Settings

Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target | 100, **150**, 200 | The score that ends the game at the end of a round. |
| Power cards | Off, **Classic** | Off is pure flip-or-save. A good first game. |

## On your screen

- **Header.** The round, the heat limit for the round, how many cards are left in the deck, and the target.
- **One row per player.** Name, score, and a chip: Flipping, Saved +N, Perfect +N, Too hot or Left. A burn icon with a count shows their Burns. "Next ×2" means a Double is waiting.
- **Heat bar.** It fills as heat goes up and turns from blue to red. The line is that player's limit, with the numbers next to it (`17 / 24`). After a Limit down, the line moves left. When a player is 3 or less away from the limit, the bar shakes.
- **Cards.** Each card shows its number, its colour and a shape: red circle, amber triangle, blue square, green diamond. A "×2" mark means it counts double.
- **Buttons.** Flip and Save +N, where N is what you would score now. Save is greyed out on your first turn.
- **Aiming.** For Push and Burn, the rows you can aim at get a red border and the word "Pick". Pick one. For Swap, pick one of your cards and one of theirs.
- **Log.** One line under the table says what just happened, like "Asha pushed a Red 8 onto you."
- Sounds and vibration mark flips, saves, going too hot, power cards and your turn.

## When something goes wrong

- **Idle player.** The host gets a "Skip" button after a while. A skip saves for them, or flips if it's their first turn. If they were aiming a card, the game picks a target (and cards) at random.
- **Disconnected player.** The game waits, and their row shows them as reconnecting. The host can skip or remove them.
- **The host removes a player.** They're out. If they were aiming a card, the card does nothing and play goes on. If only one player is left, the game ends.
- **No cards left at all.** A Flip saves you. A Push does nothing.
- **Game over.** The host sees Rematch and Back to lobby; everyone else sees Play again.

## Tips

- With 5 to go, about 1 flip in 3 makes you too hot. With 3 to go, more than half do.
- Colour bonuses add up. 3 reds and one of each other colour is +10.
- Watch other players' bars. A Push onto someone at 26 of 27 is very likely to make them too hot.
