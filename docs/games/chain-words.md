# Chain Words

Each word starts with the last letter of the one before. On the clock. 2–12 players.

## The short version
- One chain of words, built by everyone in turn. Each word has to start with the last letter of the previous one.
- On your go, type a word before your clock runs out.
- Run out of time and you lose a life. Lose them all and you're out.
- Last player standing wins.

## How it plays
1. The game opens with a random word (ROCKET, GARDEN, TIGER...). The player to the dealer's left goes first, and has to start with the opener's last letter.
2. If it's your go, the status line reads "Your go: a word starting with R" and the input box wakes up. Everyone else sees whose go it is, with the clock running.
3. Type a word and tap **Play**. If it's good, it joins the chain and the next living player gets a full clock and the new last letter.
4. A bad word bounces with the reason. The clock keeps going, so try again.
5. If the clock hits zero, you lose a life. The next player takes the same letter, with a full clock.
6. Keep going until one player has lives left.

A word counts if it:
- is letters only, 3 to 30 letters;
- starts with the required letter;
- is in the dictionary;
- hasn't been played this game, including the opener.

Case doesn't matter.

If a letter runs out of unused words, the game swaps it for a random one that still has some ("Nothing left on X. Y instead"). Nobody loses a life for that.

## Winning and scoring
There are no points. The last player with a life wins. The results list shows how long everyone lasted ("first out", "out 2nd"...), lives left for the winner, and how many words each player added to the chain. Players who left the room are listed last.

If the dictionary somehow runs dry, the game ends and everyone still alive wins.

## Settings
Host picks these in the lobby.

| Setting | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clock | 5 s, **8 s**, 12 s | Seconds you get on each turn. |
| Lives | 1, 2, **3** | How many slow turns you can take before you're out. |

## On your screen
- **The letter:** big, in capitals. That's what your word starts with.
- **The chain:** a strip of the last 12 words with who played each (the opener is labelled "Opener"). The count above it says how many words the chain has in all.
- **The clock:** seconds left and a bar. The label shows the full turn length, for example "8 s a turn".
- **Status line:** "Your go: ..." or "<name>'s go".
- **Typing:** what the current player is typing shows live to everyone. A bounced word shakes there too.
- **Input and Play button:** only usable on your go. If you're out, you're watching.
- **Players:** lives as pips, a highlight on whoever's up, and words each player has added.
- **News line:** after a timeout, "Time. <name> loses a life" or "... is out", and the letter swap message.
- **Sounds:** a tick as the clock runs low, a chime on your go, a bang on a timeout. Your phone buzzes on your go and a timeout.

## When things go sideways
- **Time runs out:** you lose a life and the turn moves on. The letter stays the same for the next player.
- **Someone disconnects:** they're passed over when the turn moves, as long as someone else is connected. If it's their go, the clock keeps running and the host can skip them.
- **Someone is removed by the host:** they're out. If it was their go, the turn passes on with a fresh clock.
- **Down to one player:** the game ends right away.
- **Play again:** starts a new chain with the same settings and a new opener.

## Tips
- Words ending in X, Q or Z are awkward to follow. Hand them to the next player when you can.
- Played words are gone for everyone, so save a good one you've spotted for when the common ones are used up.
