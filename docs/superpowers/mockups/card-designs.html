What I think

  • Overheat and Not My Problem!: bold. These cards are new to players. A big icon and a colour tell you what a card does in a glance, even at hand size, where the rule text is too small to read anyway. Tap a card to see its rule.
  • The normal deck: printed, with the four-colour suits. People already know how playing cards look, so the printed style reads as "real cards" right away. The four colours fix the one real problem today: hearts and diamonds look the same on a small screen.
  • One look for the whole app: if you want every game to feel the same, go bold everywhere. It is simpler to draw, and it fits the app's flat dark style better than paper cards do.
  • Cost: one shared card piece for the 8 normal-deck games, then a drawn icon for each of the 20 special cards. Text stays the same, and all icons are SVG.
