These are the rules for using CouchParty. By using the site, you agree to them. We keep them short and plain.

Who can use CouchParty

You must be 13 or older to use CouchParty. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before you buy Host Pass.

How to behave

Be kind to the people in your room. Do not:

  • share hate, threats, sexual content or anything that breaks the law, in names, chat or drawings;
  • bully or harass other players;
  • make lots of rooms to spam, or use bots or scripts on the site;
  • try to break, overload or avoid the limits of the site.

The host can remove players from a room. We can block people who break these rules.

Your account

Only hosts need an account. You sign in with Google or with a link we send to your email. Keep your email account safe, because anyone who can open it can sign in as you.

You can delete your account at any time on the account page.

Host Pass

Host Pass gives the host extra games and bigger rooms. It covers everyone in rooms the host starts.

We may give Host Pass for free with a code. We can end free or paid Host Pass if it is used for abuse, such as sharing codes for sale or breaking the rules above.

If your pass ends while a game is running, that room keeps its Host Pass until it closes.

Payments and refunds

Buying Host Pass is not open yet. When it opens, payments will go through a payment provider. Refunds will follow that provider's rules, and we will link them here.

The games

We try to keep CouchParty running, but we cannot promise it will always work. Rooms close when everyone leaves. Games can change, and we may add or remove games.

We are not responsible for what players type or draw in a room.
