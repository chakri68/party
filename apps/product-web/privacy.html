Privacy

Last updated 11 Nov 2026.

We keep as little as we can. This page lists all of it.

Players

If you join a room as a player, we store nothing about you. Your name and chat live only in the room while it is open. When the room closes, they are gone.

Sign-in links

When you ask for a sign-in link, we keep your email with that link. We delete it about one day after the link stops working.

Hosts with an account

If you sign in, we store:

  • your email address, so you can sign in;
  • your plan, and when it ends;
  • the codes you redeemed;
  • a sign-in cookie, so you stay signed in for 30 days.

We do not store passwords. For a sign-in link, we keep your email and a hashed copy of the link (a one-way code made from it), not the link itself. For the sign-in cookie, we keep only a hashed copy.

Rooms

When anyone starts a room, we note the room code, the time, and a hashed copy of the IP address. We use this to stop room spam: each IP address can only start a few free rooms in 6 hours. We delete these room records about one day later.

Who helps us run the site

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the games. It also runs the human check (Turnstile) and simple visit counts with no cookies (Web Analytics).
  • Resend sends sign-in emails.
  • Google signs you in, only if you choose Sign in with Google.
  • Google Fonts sends the page's font, so Google sees your IP address when a page loads.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads.

Delete your data

Go to the account page and tap Delete account. This deletes your email, your plan, your codes and your sign-in links at once.

Sign-in link records are deleted on their own about one day after the link stops working, even if you never signed in.

Your rights

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) and the EU's GDPR give you rights over your data. You can ask us to show you what we hold, fix it, or delete it. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
