Privacy

Last updated 12 Nov 2026.

We keep as little about you as we can. This page lists all of it.

Players

If you join a room as a player, we keep nothing about you. Your name and chat stay in the room only while it is open. When the room closes, they are gone.

Sign-in links

When you ask for a sign-in link, we keep your email with that link. We delete it about one day after the link stops working.

Hosts with an account

If you sign in, we keep:

  • your email, so you can sign in
  • your plan, and when it ends
  • the codes you used
  • for each pass you buy: the plan, the price, and the payment number from Razorpay or Paddle
  • a sign-in cookie, so you stay signed in for 30 days

We never see or keep your card or bank details.

We do not keep passwords. There are none. For sign-in links and the sign-in cookie, we keep a scrambled copy that cannot be turned back into the real thing.

Rooms

When anyone starts a room, we note the room code, the time, and a scrambled copy of the IP address. This stops spam: one IP address can start only a few free rooms in 6 hours. We delete these notes after about one day.

Who helps us run the site

  • Cloudflare runs the site and the games. It also does the "are you human" check (Turnstile) and counts visits without cookies (Web Analytics).
  • Resend sends the sign-in emails.
  • Google signs you in, only if you pick Sign in with Google.
  • Razorpay takes payments in India. It gets your payment details and email. We only get told that you paid, how much, and a payment number.
  • Paddle takes payments outside India. It sells to you on our behalf and handles tax. It gets your name, email, country and payment details. We only get told that you paid, how much, and an order number.
  • Google Fonts sends the font this site uses. So Google sees your IP address when a page opens.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads.

Delete your data

Go to the account page and tap Delete account. Your email, plan, codes and sign-in links are deleted right away.

Records of passes you bought stay, but without your email. Tax rules say we must keep them.

Old sign-in links delete themselves about one day after they stop working, even if you never used them.

Your rights

Indian law (the DPDP Act) and European law (GDPR) give you rights over your data. You can ask us to show you what we keep, fix it, or delete it. If you are not happy with how we handle it, you can complain to the data protection office in your country.
