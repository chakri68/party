How it works

It takes about a minute.

  1. Start a room.

    Tap Start a room and pick a game. You do not need an account.

  2. Send the link in your group chat.

    Each room has a short link and a code. Your friends tap it to join.

  3. Everyone plays on their phone.

    Sit together on the couch or play over a video call.

Questions

Do my friends need an account?

No. They open your link, type a name, and play. Only a host with Host Pass signs in.

Do I need to download anything?

No. CouchParty works in the web browser on any phone, tablet or computer.

How many people can play?

It depends on the game. Free rooms take up to 8 players. With Host Pass, some games take up to 16.

What is Host Pass?

Host Pass is a plan for the host. It opens the big games and bigger rooms for everyone in your room. Players never pay.

Is it free?

Yes. Most games are free, and you do not need an account. Host Pass is only for the extra games and features.
