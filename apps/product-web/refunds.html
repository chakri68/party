Cancellation and refunds

Last updated 12 Nov 2026.

If Host Pass did not work out for you, we want to give your money back. This page tells you how. It is part of our terms.

Full refund in 14 days

For a month or year pass, ask within 14 days of paying. We give back the full amount. You do not have to tell us why.

A day pass lasts only 24 hours. We refund it if something went wrong. For example, the games did not open, the room did not work, or you paid by mistake. Ask within 14 days of paying.

We always refund when

  • you paid twice for the same thing
  • you paid, but your account did not get Host Pass

Nothing renews

You pay once. Host Pass does not renew, and we never charge you again on our own. When it ends, your new rooms are free rooms until you buy again. So there is nothing to cancel.

How to ask

Email hello@couchpartygames.com from the email you sign in with. Add the payment receipt if you have it. We reply within 2 working days.

When you get the money

In India, you paid through Razorpay. After we say yes, the money reaches your bank, card or UPI in 5 to 7 working days.

Outside India, our order process is run by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all those orders. So Paddle sends the refund. It reaches you in 5 to 10 working days, based on your bank. You can also ask Paddle directly at paddle.net.

The money goes back the same way you paid.

After a refund

Host Pass ends on your account when the refund is done. A game that is already running keeps going until the room closes.

If you got your pass free with a code, there is nothing to refund.
