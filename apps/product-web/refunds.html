Cancellation and refunds

Last updated 12 Nov 2026.

If Host Pass did not work for you, we want to give your money back. This page says how. It is part of our terms.

Full refund in 14 days

For a month or year pass, ask within 14 days of paying and we refund the full amount. You do not need to give a reason.

A day pass lasts only 24 hours, so we refund it when something went wrong: the games did not open, the room did not work, or you paid by mistake. Ask within 14 days of paying.

Always refunded

  • You paid twice for the same thing.
  • You paid, but your account did not get Host Pass.

Nothing renews

Host Pass is a one-time payment. It does not renew and we never charge you again on our own. When it ends, your new rooms are free rooms until you buy another pass. There is nothing to cancel.

How to ask

Email hello@couchpartygames.com from the email you sign in with. Add the payment receipt if you have it. We reply within 2 working days.

When the money comes back

In India, payments go through Razorpay. Once we approve a refund, it reaches your bank, card or UPI account in 5 to 7 working days.

Outside India, our order process is run by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all those orders: it takes the payment, handles tax and makes the refund. It reaches you in 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank. You can also ask Paddle directly at paddle.net.

The money goes back the same way you paid.

After a refund

Host Pass ends on your account when the refund is made. A game that is already running keeps going until the room closes.

A pass you got with a free code has nothing to refund.
