Cancellation and refunds

Last updated 12 Nov 2026.

If Host Pass did not work for you, we want to give your money back. This page says how.

Full refund in 7 days

For a month or year pass, ask within 7 days of paying and we refund the full amount. You do not need to give a reason.

A day pass lasts only 24 hours, so we refund it when something went wrong: the games did not open, the room did not work, or you paid by mistake. Ask within 7 days of paying.

Always refunded

  • You paid twice for the same thing.
  • You paid, but your account did not get Host Pass.
  • A plan renewed and you did not mean it to. Ask within 7 days of the renewal.

Cancelling

A day pass does not renew. If a month or year plan renews, you can stop it at any time. You keep Host Pass until the end of the time you already paid for, and you are not charged again.

How to ask

Email hello@couchpartygames.com from the email you sign in with. Add the payment receipt if you have it. We reply within 2 working days.

When the money comes back

In India, payments go through Razorpay. Once we approve a refund, it reaches your bank, card or UPI account in 5 to 7 working days.

Outside India, Paddle sells Host Pass for us and handles the refund. It reaches you in 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank.

The money goes back the same way you paid.

After a refund

Host Pass ends on your account when the refund is made. A game that is already running keeps going until the room closes.

A pass you got with a free code has nothing to refund.
